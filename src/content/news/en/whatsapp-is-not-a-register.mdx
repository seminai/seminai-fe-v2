It is seven in the evening. The person who sprayed sends a voice note: “Did the lower block, the usual, two hours.” The office answers with a thumb. The voice note did its human job: it warned, it closed the day, it took a worry away.

Three months later that voice note is not enough. Which product. Which dose. Which part of the lower block, if the lower block is four hectares and a strip along the water. If the person who sprayed is away, or does not remember, **the notebook line is born from an approximation**.

WhatsApp, or whatever chat you use, is an excellent place to talk. It is **a poor register**, if you leave it as it is.

## Why chat beats the notebook

The official notebook lives in the office. The spray lives outside. Asking the person in the cab to fill software with busy hands produces two results: they do not do it, or they do it badly and fast. The phone is already in the pocket. The message leaves in ten seconds. That is why advisers ask for it, and why technicians accept it.

The trouble is not the message. It is **the idea that the message is already the data**. In the chat, by turns, the full trade name is missing, the quantity that left the tank is missing, the area is missing, the confirmation that the product was in stock is missing. The tone of someone who knows the farm is there. The sentence an inspection can read is not.

Then there is who keeps it. The chat sits on one person’s phone. The farm does not have an ordered copy by plot and by date. If that person changes phone, or role, **the season disappears with the voice notes**.

## A suggestion is not yet a line

<ResearchNote
  label="What the research says"
  source="Amershi and colleagues, 2019. Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction. CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems."
  href="https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300233"
>
Guidelines from a Microsoft research group, written for people who design systems that assist others, insist on two simple moves. The system should make clear what it is suggesting. And it should let a person correct quickly, before the result becomes the final one. A voice note turned into a register line with no rereading does the opposite: it presents as fact something that was only a warning.
</ResearchNote>

The guidelines are not about farming or about chat. They say how to behave when a machine helps write a document that someone will later sign. The register is exactly that kind of document.

## From the message to the line, with a pause

The sound path has three moments. You capture at once, from the field, in the words that come. You suggest a line: probable field, probable product, quantity still to complete. You confirm, by the person who takes responsibility, after seeing what is missing.

The pause in the middle **is not bureaucracy**. It is the point where “the usual” becomes a name, and “the lower block” becomes a plot with its sprayed area. Without that pause, a single archive would fill with sentences, not with facts.

## What to ask in the message, without turning it into a form

You can stay human and still leave the four pieces. One guiding sentence, sent once to the group, is enough: **field, product, how much, today**. It is not a form. It is the minimum you can complete in the evening without reopening the label. Whoever does not know the dose writes “full tank on the lower block” and says so. That sentence is more useful than silence, because the office knows the quantity must be rebuilt from the tank and the area, not invented.

Treat the voice note like the sentence: listen soon, pull out the line, mark what is missing. Waiting a week loses the tone that made “the usual lower block” understandable. The suggested text can also be wrong. That is why **it stays a draft**. Correcting “lower” into “south vineyard, rows 1 to 20” takes thirty seconds the next day, and half an hour in November.

There is finally the question of which thing is the register and which is the chat. The chat can remain the place where you warn each other. The farm archive is the place where the confirmed line lives even if the phone changes. Copying it by hand from the chat to the notebook, once a week, is already better than not copying it. Letting it be born as a draft in the same flow, with a check, removes that copy too. In both cases the message is not, by itself, the document.

A practical warning: do not rebuild the group every season “because the old one is unreadable”. The group can stay the channel. What has to be pulled out, within a few days, is the line. If at month end the register holds the sprays and the chat holds only the chatter, you used the right tool for each thing. If at month end the chat is the only place where you can tell what was done, the register is still a future task, and in farming the future always arrives too late.

Seminai treats notes as material to read, not as a register already closed. The official line **comes after confirmation**. We do not promise that every chat on a personal phone is already connected. We do promise that what enters the farm archive does not skip the check.
